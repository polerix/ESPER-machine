## De-music procedure

1. Mark the Final Cut picture event and make a private analysis window around it.
2. Align the mixed reference with a candidate clean music cue by transient/musical landmark; record offset, polarity and gain.
3. Subtract only if the cues demonstrably share the same performance and edit. If dialogue/effects smear or residual score remains, label the result `partial-match`, not “isolated.”
4. Prefer the dedicated **Esper Machine Noises** cue where it matches the film event.
5. Build an original synthetic effect from the documented behavior; source audio remains reference-only.

An event becomes `film-confirmed` only after its onset and behavior are synchronized to the Final Cut—not because a soundtrack filename names the device.
